Rules

What holds it together

Hairline surfaces

1px lines, square corners, no fills, no shadows. The two exceptions are the stone monogram block and the oxblood button.

App, not document

Header, filter bar and pager are pinned. Only the results list scrolls, with native momentum. The browser never scrolls the page.

Attention has one color

If something is oxblood, it needs you now or takes you somewhere. Nothing decorative is ever oxblood.

Plain product voice

Sentence case. The fewest words that still say what to do. No exclamation marks, no emoji, no apologies.

Ties look equal

Two listings with the same score show the same decimal. Ranks stay sequential; the engine breaks ties, the UI does not hide them.

Room to grow

A new field joins the meta line; a new filter joins the grid; a new column joins the price block. None of them needs a redesign.
