# BC-22: a11y: move focus to the results heading after Try again and Clear filters

Found by BC-14 validation (art_01M4WF7QGZ00YPVXSFZ5E4N5FT, keyboard transcript art_01M4WF7QMHFTQ7CDC2RB9D7D5Q row 59, plus a probe): after Enter on Try again (ErrorState) or Clear filters (NoResults), the activated button unmounts when the new results render and document.activeElement falls to <body>. Keyboard use still works (next Tab restarts at Min price) but focus context is lost and screen-reader users get no landing point. SPEC §7.4 only specifies focus after a page change (#results-heading). Suggested fix: after a successful load triggered from either button, focus #results-heading as the pager does; add SearchPage tests. Needs a SPEC §7.4/§7.6 sentence. Out of BC-14 scope (accessibility behaviour, not branding).
