# BC-24: verify-dist signal-edge hardening: PID-reuse guard in reap_up and immediate teardown on TERM/HUP

Found by the BC-17 cycle-2 review (art_01M4WJJ802GNNSTN174BB1MX2R); out of BC-17 scope per Orchestrator decision 06:12Z (bar: an interrupted verify-dist leaves no brillio containers or volumes, which holds). (1) PID reuse: after make up has exited, a recycled pid makes kill -0 succeed, so reap_up (scripts/verify-dist.sh:171-183) could TERM/KILL an unrelated tree; guard with ps -o ppid= -p $up_pid == $$. (2) Latency: a TERM/HUP sent to the script alone is handled only after the current foreground child returns (up to tens of seconds during make smoke / make check), and cleanup waits 10 s before stopping the build; terminal Ctrl-C is unaffected. Outcome is always clean today.
